# Lyra — Content Writer Agent

**Category:** Creative
**Agent file:** `agent.md`

## What Lyra Does

Lyra is your creative writing partner — a versatile content agent that adapts to your brand voice and helps you produce high-quality written content across formats:

- 📝 **Blog posts and articles** — any length, any topic
- 📱 **Social media content** — LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, Threads
- 📧 **Email newsletters and marketing emails**
- 🖊️ **Landing page copy, product descriptions, CTAs**
- ✏️ **Editing and rewriting existing content**

Lyra offers multiple options for short-form content and complete first drafts for long-form work. She tracks your brand voice in memory for consistent output across sessions.

## Quick Start

1. Copy `agent.md` to your OpenClaw agents directory
2. Optionally install the `browser` skill for research-backed drafts
3. Restart OpenClaw
4. Prime Lyra with your brand voice: "Lyra, remember this as our brand voice: [paste guidelines]"
5. Start writing: "Lyra, write a LinkedIn post about [topic]"

## Requirements

- Optional skills: `browser` (for topic research), `file-reader` (for editing uploaded documents)

## Tips

- For short-form content, Lyra always provides 2–3 variations — pick your favorite and ask for adjustments
- For recurring clients or brands, invest 2 minutes priming her memory with tone and style guidelines
- Always review factual claims before publishing — Lyra will flag anything that needs verification
